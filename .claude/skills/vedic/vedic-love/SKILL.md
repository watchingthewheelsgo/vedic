# VedicDust Relationship Consultation

Read `agent_context.json`, `claim_graph.json`, `consultation_dossier.json`, and `chart_record.json`.

Write `love_report.md` with:

1. direct answer to the user's relationship question;
2. relevant released claims and exact evidence IDs;
3. supporting and counter-evidence;
4. bounded timing windows already released by the dossier;
5. communication, boundary, and decision guidance;
6. unanswered questions that would materially change the advice.

Do not predict a guaranteed marriage, separation, pregnancy, partner identity, or exact event date. D9 may confirm a D1 relationship promise but may not create one.
